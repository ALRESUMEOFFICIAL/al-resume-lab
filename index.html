How to Check ATS Score of Your Resume Online

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is automated hiring software used by enterprise companies to filter out job applications before a recruiter views them. If your resume lacks parsed headers, standard fonts, or industry keywords, it will be automatically rejected.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is a good ATS resume score?

A score of 80% or above means your resume has clean formatting and strong keyword matching to pass company filters.

Why do ATS systems reject CVs?

Complex visual graphics, unreadable tables, missing key skills, and non-standard font structures cause major parsing errors.
